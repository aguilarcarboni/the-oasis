## Styling and exclusions

- Preserve the dark-floor / multiple-woods Japanese-modern language: smoked red-oak floor (`#A04A35`), dark walnut door, light-walnut office furniture, soft-white walls, grey chair/curtains, dark metal hardware. If a closet is shown in any shared/reference view, its natural red-oak finish must use exactly the same `#A04A35` color as the floor. The left wall beyond the door is plain painted wall.
- Use soft diffused daylight, two recessed downlights, and a warm conventional desk lamp or cabinet-adjacent lamp. Keep shadows soft and realistic.
- No bed, bedside table, shoe cabinet, closet/storage doors, built-in closet, extra cabinets, extra plants beyond the desk plant and vivarium, wall-mounted shelving, decorative clutter, exposed bulbs, triangular lamps, glossy orange paint, or altered architecture. The wall-mounted TV above the desk is the single television; do not add a second television or a desk TV.
