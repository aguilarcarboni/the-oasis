# Modern Minimalist Warm Contemporary

This style is a blend of modern minimalism, warm Scandinavian influences, and lightweight contemporary design. It’s clean, streamlined, airy, and uncluttered, but not cold—it uses warm tones and natural textures to keep the spaces comfortable and inviting.
Below is the full breakdown.

## Color Palette
### Base Colors
* Soft beige
* Darker beige
* Gray
* Natural wood tones on the lighter side
* Black
* White

### Accent Colors (in artwork or decor)
* Vibrant but tasteful pops: bright red, orange, teal, blue, etc
* Lots of green in plants

## Floors
* Light warm beige wood or ceramic that looks like wood for bedrooms and bathrooms.
* White shiny ceramic floors for the living room, etc
* The goal is a neutral, continuous floor that acts as a clean backdrop, without drawing attention.

## Walls
* Satin very soft off-white.
* Sometimes a subtle warm darker beige accent wall.

## Furniture Style and wood elements
* Mostly light natural or white painted wood.
* Warm undertones (honey, sand, warm blonde), never reddish or dark heavy walnut.
* Matte or barely satin—no shine.
* Clean, simple shapes without ornate details.
* Low-profile, modern silhouettes.
* Straight lines with gentle curves.
* No ornate details, minimal hardware.
* Floating or wall-mounted items (like the TV).
* Long, low TV tables with decor and media consoles.
* Bedside tables with clean lines, often white.
* Coffee tables
* Couches arranged for both comfort and symmetry.

## Metal Accents
* Black matte metal.
* Used sparingly, typically in:
    * table legs
    * lamp frames
    * curtain rods
    * small decor objects

## Textiles and Fabrics
* Cotton
* Linen
* Wool blends
* Soft woven throws
* Fluffy white bedding.
* Neutral-colored rugs with light patterns.
* Thick, modern curtains in solid tones (gray).

## Lights
* Warm LED bulbs (2700K–3000K).
* Fixtures use simple modern shapes:
    * frosted glass
    * warm light pendants
    * black or brushed metal

## Decor
* Large, colorful statement pieces above beds or sofas.
* Typically abstract, nature landscapes, or geometric—never overly detailed or classic.
* Green plants in minimalistic pots to break the neutral palette and add life.
* Very few accessories:
    * candles in glass
    * wooden trays
    * one or two curated objects
    * books with clean covers
